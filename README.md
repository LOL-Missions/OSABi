## Vision

A significant share of the world cannot read or write — and their cultures are
oral, not written. A written Bible, handed to them, is useless. At the same
time, Bible apps assume internet, cell service, and reliable power that much
of the world simply doesn't have.

So OSABi is an audio Bible: a real device, offline, in a person's own
language, that just plays Scripture when you press a button.

The first person to hear one was **Zulay**, a Quechua woman from the northern
Andes of Ecuador. She was nearly 70 years old — not living in a remote
village, but with a cell phone, electricity, and internet — and it was the
first time in her life she had ever heard the Bible in her own language. She
nearly wept, and immediately went to tell her neighbors.

OSABi exists for every other Zulay in this world — and for everyone with even
less access than she had. As Light of Life Missions puts it: **faith comes
from hearing** (Romans 10:17).

## Features

- 📖 On-screen **book, chapter, and verse** — shown in the same language as
  the recording, with a battery indicator
- 🔘 Simple side buttons: **volume**, **arrows** to find any verse, and one
  **Play** button
- ▶️ Press Play for a single verse · **hold Play** for continuous listening
- 🔋 Runs on standard replaceable batteries — no charging cables, no solar
  panels, nothing to fail in the field
- 🌍 Change the language, not the device: each Bible version is a pack of
  files you copy onto a microSD card and slide in
- 💰 Target build cost: **under $10 per unit** — and less in bulk
- - 🎧 Headphone jack — for private listening or sharing with a friend

*(Hardware is being built now; details may evolve with v1.)*

## Content & Canon

OSABi plays the **complete Protestant Bible — all 66 books**.

**The text of Scripture is never altered.** No books are added, no books are
removed, no wording is changed. OSABi content distributed on this page is
always a faithful, word-for-word Protestant Bible.

- **Public domain only (here):** everything published in this repository is a
  public-domain translation — free for anyone to copy, duplicate, and share
  with zero restrictions or royalties
- **An open format, not a walled garden:** the OSABi file format is fully
  documented so translation organizations — Wycliffe, Bible Societies, Faith
  Comes By Hearing, and others — can produce OSABi-ready Bibles in the
  languages they already serve
- The player will play whatever Bible pack you load onto it — but the OSABi
  name, and the content hosted here, travels only with the 66-book Protestant
  canon
