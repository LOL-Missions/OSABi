## Vision

**739 million adults worldwide cannot read or write** — and many of them
belong to oral cultures where truth is carried by voice, not text. A printed
Bible handed to them goes unused. Meanwhile, Bible apps assume internet,
cell service, and reliable power that much of the world simply doesn't have.

So OSABi is an audio Bible: a real, offline device that plays Scripture in a
person's own language at the press of a button.

The person who inspired OSABi was a Quechua woman from the northern Andes of
Ecuador — we'll call her **Zulay**. She was nearly 70 years old, and it was
the first time in her life she had ever heard the Bible in her own language.
She nearly wept. And she wasn't off the grid — she had a cell phone,
electricity, and internet — proof that connectivity was never the missing
piece. *Language was.*

OSABi exists for every other Zulay in this world — and for everyone with
even less access than she had. As Light of Life Missions (LOL) believes:
**faith comes from hearing** (Romans 10:17).

<sub>Source: UNESCO Institute for Statistics, 2025 — two-thirds of these
739 million adults are women.</sub>

## Features

- 📖 On-screen **book, chapter, and verse** — shown in the same language as
  the recording, with a battery indicator
- 🔘 Simple side buttons: **volume**, **arrows** to find any verse, and one
  **Play** button
- ▶️ Press Play for a single verse · **hold Play** for continuous listening
- 🎧 Headphone jack — for private listening or sharing with a friend
- 🔋 Runs on standard replaceable batteries — no charging cables, no solar
  panels, nothing to fail in the field
- 🌍 Change the language, not the device: each Bible version is a pack of
  files you copy onto a microSD card and slide in
- 💰 Target build cost: **under $10 per unit** — and less in bulk

*(Hardware is being built now; details may evolve with v1.)*

## Content & Canon

OSABi plays the **complete Protestant Bible — all 66 books**.

**The text of Scripture is never altered.** No books are added, no books are
removed, no wording is changed. OSABi content distributed on this page is
always a faithful, word-for-word Protestant Bible.

- **Public domain only (here):** everything published in this repository is
  a public-domain translation — free for anyone to copy, duplicate, and
  share with zero restrictions or royalties
- **An open format, not a walled garden:** the OSABi file format is fully
  documented so translation organizations — Wycliffe, Bible Societies,
  Faith Comes By Hearing, and others — can produce OSABi-ready Bibles in
  the languages they already serve
- The player will play whatever Bible pack you load onto it — but the
  OSABi name, and the content hosted here, travels only with the 66-book
  Protestant canon
